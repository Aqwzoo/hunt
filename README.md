You were not sent this. You found it.

That distinction matters more than it seems.

Somewhere behind the image you are looking at is a number. It was
given to you and to no one else in quite the same way. Numbers are
not answers. They are the beginning of a question you have not yet
learned to ask.

Submit it. Then look at what comes back to you — not the score,
the *residue*. Most who reach this point look only at whether they
were right. You will need to look at what was left behind instead.
It is not written in a language you already read. It will take
patience before it takes cleverness.

We do not do this for glory, and neither should you. There is no
leaderboard that matters here, only the quiet knowledge of who
finished and who stopped early.

─────────────────────────────

A few truths, before you go further:

**We give nothing away that force can take faster than thought.**
If you find yourself guessing at random, or reading source code
looking for a shortcut, or trying every short word you can think
of — stop. That is not the door. The door only opens for the
answer, never for the lock itself. Anyone clever enough to reach
here is clever enough to find the real way through.

**What you learn, you may keep. What you are given, you may not
sell.** Share your progress with no one who has not earned it
themselves. A key spoken aloud in a crowded room stops being a key.

**Some doors do not open on the first knock, no matter the hour.**
If a door tells you it is sealed, it is not broken — it is waiting
for a time already written into it. Come back then. This is not
cruelty. It is the only way many of you arrive at the end together
rather than scattered across three days, alone.

**If you are stuck and truly stuck** — not impatient, but stuck —
there is a way to ask without giving up: [insert your hint channel
here]. Asking is not failure. Quitting quietly is the only failure
we recognize.

**This is watched over by someone.** [Sponsor / teacher name] knows
this exists and has seen what is asked of you. Nothing here is
without an adult's knowledge, whatever else it may look like.

**What we keep of you is small and stated plainly, not hidden in
fine print:** [state exactly what your Form actually collects — a
name, a school email, nothing at all — and who besides you can see
it]. We do not pretend to secrecy we cannot promise. If this runs
through a school account, the school can see what a school account
always can.
